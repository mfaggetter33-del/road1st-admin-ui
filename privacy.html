ROAD 1ST

Privacy Policy

How ROAD 1ST uses information in the Android driving safety app and optional fleet services.

Effective 5 November 2026

1. Who provides ROAD 1ST

ROAD 1ST is the trading name used for an Ireland based independent driving safety application. For questions about personal data and privacy rights, contact info@r1st.ie. Business correspondence: ROAD 1ST, Unit 19, Tom Crean Business Centre, Dromthacker, Tralee, County Kerry, Ireland.

For ordinary private app use and ROAD 1ST's own support, security and optional research purposes, ROAD 1ST determines the purposes of relevant processing. For fleet operational records, the enrolled fleet organisation normally determines why records are used and who is permitted to access them. ROAD 1ST provides technical processing services on its behalf. Your fleet organisation should provide its own driver privacy information.

2. What the app uses on your phone

The app stores settings needed to run the service, including a local driver profile and PIN verification information, saved vehicle Bluetooth links, driving restriction preferences, selected language and terms acceptance. With permissions granted through Android, it can use Bluetooth connection information, GPS based movement, speed and accuracy readings, app usage access, camera access, notifications and an on screen blocking overlay.

App usage access lets ROAD 1ST identify the app in the foreground to apply the restrictions you select. It is not permission to read messages, email contents or the contents of other apps. GPS movement readings are used to determine whether the vehicle is moving and whether automatic protection can be dismissed while parked. A Bluetooth connection by itself does not create a recorded journey.

3. Private drivers and optional research

ROAD 1ST does not retain identifiable private journey histories, routes, precise locations, names or saved Bluetooth device identifiers in its backend for private driving. Vehicle links, any local driver profile and safety preferences needed for the app remain on your phone, and are not a remotely retained private driver database. Optional private driving research is off by default. If you separately choose to join, the app may send combined counts for groups of at least three private journeys over Wi Fi, including duration bands, Bluetooth availability, temporary stops, unusual endings and software version bands. These research payloads are designed not to include your name, vehicle registration, installation ID, exact route, GPS coordinates or precise journey times.

After six private journeys, the app may invite you to reconsider optional research. A separate voluntary count only submission can occur only if you actively choose it. You can turn optional research off; outstanding local aggregate counters are then cleared. Previously aggregated server totals cannot normally be separated back into individual journeys. Technical hosting systems may process network metadata such as IP addresses.

4. Additional data when using Fleet mode

For authorised fleet operations, ROAD 1ST may process driver and fleet identifiers, device and installation identifiers, vehicle registrations, enrolment and status information, journey and shift events, vehicle checks, odometer readings, defect details and photographs, fuel information and receipt photographs, and technical diagnostics such as app version, device status and GPS speed quality readings. A receipt or user supplied photograph or note can contain additional personal information visible in that content.

Selected fleet app blocking diagnostics can record app names or package identifiers and blocking events. A linked two phone crew can exchange necessary driver and journey controls over Bluetooth. Authorised personnel of your fleet organisation may view the records for their own fleet. ROAD 1ST records are not a substitute for a statutory tachograph or employer compliance duties.

The current service can record fleet journey information for eligible vehicle types. Planned changes to make certain non tachograph Car or Van estimates display only by default are not represented here as already operational.

5. Why information is used and legal grounds

Private app preferences and local safety controls are used to provide the functions you request. Where ROAD 1ST independently handles personal information, applicable legal grounds may include providing a requested service, legitimate interests in keeping it secure and responding to support requests, complying with legal obligations, or your separate consent for optional private research. You may withdraw optional research consent at any time without preventing ordinary private use.

Fleet organisations are responsible for identifying and explaining the lawful grounds for their employment, driver and vehicle processing. ROAD 1ST processes fleet information to provide the fleet services on the organisation's instructions. Agreeing to app terms or allowing Android permissions is not blanket consent to employment monitoring.

6. Who may receive information

Fleet records may be available to authorised personnel of the enrolled fleet organisation. ROAD 1ST uses technical service providers to operate secure backend, storage, hosting, support and communications functions. The inspected backend uses Supabase, including database and private storage services in its EU West project. Limited data may be supplied to professional advisers or public authorities when required by law. ROAD 1ST does not use private journey information for advertising or sell it to advertisers.

Some technical processing, access or logging may take place outside the European Economic Area depending on provider services and support arrangements. Where a transfer is subject to GDPR transfer rules, appropriate safeguards are required. For details relevant to a particular service or request, contact info@r1st.ie.

7. Security

The app's inspected fleet API connections use HTTPS, and fleet photographs and report storage are held in private storage areas. Access to fleet records is intended for authorised organisations and users. No service can guarantee absolute security. Bluetooth crew connections, third party infrastructure and backup handling are separate technical systems; this notice does not claim that every possible transfer has been independently verified as encrypted.

8. Retention and deletion

Fleet retention policy: ROAD 1ST aims to retain each category of fleet record only for the minimum period required by the legislation actually applicable to that record, or, where no statutory minimum applies, only for as long as necessary for its documented lawful purpose. The fleet organisation remains responsible for determining which records it must keep and issuing documented retention instructions. There is no universal three year rule for all fleet data.

For Irish operators, examples of legally required minimum periods, only when the record qualifies, include:

  • Commercial vehicle inspection, maintenance, repair and defect records: at least two years after creation under the commercial vehicle roadworthiness rules. See CVRT recordkeeping guidance.
  • Road transport working time records: at least three years after the period covered for employers under the amended mobile road transport working time rules (S.I. 49 of 2015 amending S.I. 36 of 2012). Some records may also fall under other statutory regimes. See the 2015 amendment.
  • Actual statutory tachograph data: at least twelve months, where applicable. ROAD 1ST's estimated journey and break information is not an approved tachograph record and does not automatically require tachograph retention. See RSA tachograph guidance.
  • Tax or accounting evidence, including qualifying fuel receipts: generally six years under Irish tax recordkeeping rules, when those documents are used for tax purposes. See Revenue recordkeeping guidance.

Other fleet records such as app connection logs, non statutory estimated journeys and technical diagnostics are not given an invented legal minimum. They should be deleted when their lawful operational need ends. Records may need to be kept longer for a valid legal hold, ongoing investigation or a separate statutory obligation, but not simply for convenience. The current cloud service does not implement automatic category based purging; retention and deletion are handled under documented fleet instructions and require operational verification. The fourteen day app startup cleanup applies only to certain unscoped local files, not cloud fleet records.

Private retention: ROAD 1ST does not keep an identifiable private journey history in its backend. Where you separately opt in, only coarse combined app improvement statistics, such as journey counts, duration categories, Bluetooth connection availability and app version bands, are retained as monthly research totals. Research payloads do not contain personal or vehicle identifiers, exact times or routes. The app keeps local settings while needed for its operation; turning off optional research clears outstanding local research counts. Hosting and support systems may separately hold limited technical request metadata for security and service operation, subject to their own necessity and retention controls.

Uninstalling the app does not erase lawfully held fleet records. Deletion requests are assessed with the responsible fleet organisation, and relevant data is removed when no longer lawfully required. Backups and legal holds may delay complete removal when permitted by law. The support address is a deletion request route, not an automatic deletion system.

Uninstalling ROAD 1ST does not erase data already lawfully held for a fleet. We assess valid deletion requests and, for fleet information, work with the responsible organisation. Where deletion is required, data is removed or scheduled for removal from relevant systems subject to mandatory retention and lawful exceptions. Do not assume that deleting a local profile automatically closes a fleet account.

9. Your choices and rights

You can manage Android permissions in phone settings and optional private research through the app. Depending on your circumstances and applicable GDPR conditions, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing. You may also withdraw any optional consent you have given.

Email info@r1st.ie with the subject “ROAD 1ST Privacy Request”. State whether the matter concerns private use or an identified fleet. Do not include your password or PIN. We may need to verify your identity. If a fleet organisation is responsible for the relevant data, ROAD 1ST may refer the request to it and assist with its response. Some deletion requests may be limited by a legal obligation or valid legal hold.

10. Requests for account deletion

To request deletion of a ROAD 1ST account or personal information, email info@r1st.ie with the subject “ROAD 1ST Account Deletion Request”. Explain which app profile or fleet account is involved. We will explain any information we must retain and direct fleet related requests to the organisation responsible. This is a request pathway, not an automated account deletion service.

11. Complaints and changes

You can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at www.dataprotection.ie. We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or data practices change. The revised date will appear on this page. If a change materially affects how personal information is used, we will provide further notice where required by law.
