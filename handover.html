Fleet handover

Checking authorised ROAD 1ST session…

A customer must download their records and personally acknowledge receipt before ROAD 1ST can delete anything. The legal retention checks and owner approval are separate protections.

Checking status…

1. Prepare customer handover · ROAD 1ST owner only

Exports fleet-scoped journey reports, fuel receipts, defect photographs, driver/vehicle lists, work events and configured fleet settings to an encrypted-access private Storage package. Individual file and package SHA-256 checksums are included. It does not include stored security-token hashes.

If records exceed the package safety limit, the operation stops rather than silently omit records. The original records remain untouched.

2. Download and verify the records

—

Your browser checks the downloaded file against the server's SHA-256 checksum.

3. Customer confirmation · Fleet manager only

The authorised fleet manager must confirm they have saved, inspected and can open their copy. The ROAD 1ST platform owner cannot sign this step for the customer.

4. Owner approval and verified close

Checking retention and privacy holds…

Closing a customer disables company access and devices, removes the specific fleet's live report files and records and retains only minimal handover/account evidence. ROAD 1ST backup expiry and legal duties are recorded separately.

Customer company access has been frozen. The next action permanently removes the fleet's original files, database records and handover ZIP from live Storage. It cannot be reversed.

Handover completed

The live deletion result is recorded. Backups and any lawful records retained by ROAD 1ST remain subject to their own retention periods.
