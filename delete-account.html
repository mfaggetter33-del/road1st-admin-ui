ROAD 1ST

Request account and data deletion

For the ROAD 1ST Android driving safety app and fleet services.

How to submit a request

You can request deletion of your ROAD 1ST account, local profile or related personal data by emailing our privacy contact. No login or paid account is required to submit the request.

Email: info@r1st.ie

Subject: ROAD 1ST Account Deletion Request

Tell us whether the request concerns private app use or a fleet account. For fleet use, include the fleet organisation’s name so we can identify the correct controller. Do not send passwords, PINs, photographs of identity documents or unnecessary personal details in your first email. We may request reasonable verification before acting.

Private motorists

Ordinary private use does not create a ROAD 1ST cloud journey history or require a ROAD 1ST online fleet account. Private driver details and linked vehicle preferences are stored locally on the phone and can be removed by clearing the app’s local storage or uninstalling the app. If you have separately opted in to app improvement research, only broad combined statistics are retained; they cannot normally be linked back to a private journey or erased as an individual journey. Technical server request metadata, where present, is handled under the Privacy Policy.

Fleet drivers and fleet accounts

Fleet identity, device enrolment, work records, defect photographs and fuel receipts may be kept in the ROAD 1ST service for the fleet organisation. If you ask to delete a fleet account or associated data, we will verify your request, identify the responsible fleet controller and assist with erasure of information no longer legally required. A fleet may need to retain statutory inspection, working time or tax records for the legally applicable minimum, or preserve records under a valid legal hold. We will explain what can and cannot be erased and why. Account deactivation alone is not treated as deletion.

What we delete and what may remain

For a valid request, personal data for which ROAD 1ST is responsible will be deleted where no legal basis for continued retention exists. For fleet records, the responsible organisation’s documented legal instructions also apply. Some information must be held for statutory recordkeeping, legal claims, fraud prevention or security; backups may take additional time to be removed under the applicable retention schedule. We do not promise that sending an email immediately deletes all data or that automated fleet deletion is already operating.

Questions or complaints

For access, correction, objection or other GDPR rights, use the same privacy contact. See our Privacy Policy for full data categories and retention criteria. You may complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.
